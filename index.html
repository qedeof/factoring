Factoring Reference Guide

Quick lookup techniques and key rules for algebraic expression factoring.

1. Greatest Common Factor (GCF)

Always the first step! Identify numbers/variables shared by every term.

$ab + ac = a(b + c)$
Ex: $6x^2 + 9x = 3x(2x + 3)$

2. Difference of Squares

Applies when subtracting two perfect squares ($a^2 - b^2$).

$a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b)$
Ex: $x^2 - 25 = (x - 5)(x + 5)$

3. Sum & Difference of Cubes

Use the SOAP rule for signs: Same, Opposite, Always Positive.

$a^3 + b^3 = (a + b)(a^2 - ab + b^2)$
$a^3 - b^3 = (a - b)(a^2 + ab + b^2)$
Ex: $x^3 - 8 = (x - 2)(x^2 + 2x + 4)$

4. Factoring 4 Terms by Grouping

Group the 4 terms into pairs, factor out GCFs, and extract the matching binomial.

$x^3 + 3x^2 + 2x + 6 = x^2(x+3) + 2(x+3)$
Result: $(x^2 + 2)(x + 3)$

Factoring Strategy Flowchart

Step 1

Pull out the Greatest Common Factor (GCF) if any exists.

Step 2: 2 Terms?

Check Difference of Squares ($a^2-b^2$) or Cubes ($a^3 \pm b^3$).

Step 3: 3 Terms?

Use product-sum strategy ($a=1$) or AC Method ($a>1$).

Step 4: 4 Terms?

Group terms in pairs, factor GCFs, and factor binomials.
